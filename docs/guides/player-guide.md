# VR Cricket League — Player Guide

How to join a tournament, play your matches and follow your results. Everything here works the
same on a phone and on a computer.

**In short:** sign up → choose a tournament → choose where you play → play one match each round,
any time before the round closes. Win and you go through; lose and you are out (with one chance to
buy back in round 1). Last player standing is the champion.

---

## 1. Sign up and log in

1. Open the site and tap **Register**.
2. Enter an email, a username (letters, numbers and `_`) and a password (8+ characters with at
   least one letter and one number).
3. Add your city and switch on **I have a VR headset** if you own a Meta Quest. This decides
   whether you can play from **Home** (see step 2 below).
4. You are taken straight to **Choose a tournament**.

Next time, just **Log in** — the app sends you to the right place automatically.

Forgot your password? Tap **Forgot password** on the login page and follow the link in the email.

## 2. The three steps

The first time you play, the app walks you through three steps. You cannot skip ahead, so there
is nothing to get lost in. The progress bar at the top shows where you are.
Diagram: [player journey](../flows/player-journey.md).

### Step 1 — Choose a tournament

You see every tournament that is still taking players. Each card shows:

- when it starts,
- how long each round lasts,
- how many players have joined (and the limit, if there is one),
- the buyback price, if buybacks are on.

Tap a tournament to choose it. Tournaments that are full or have already started are not shown.

### Step 2 — Where will you play?

Pick **one** place to play this tournament:

| Option | Who sees it | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | Players with a VR headset | You play on your own Meta Quest, wherever you are. |
| **A venue** | Everyone | You play at that arena on its headsets. Nearest venues are listed first if your location is set. |

Tap your choice, then **Join — play at …**. You can change it (or leave the tournament) any time
until the tournament starts.

### Step 3 — My tournaments

This is your home screen from now on. Each tournament you are in has its own card with one plain
sentence telling you what is happening and what to do next, for example:

- *You're in! Super Over Cup starts Sat 21 Nov, 4:00 PM.*
- *Semi-final: play zara_vr before Sun 22 Nov, 7:00 PM.*
- *You've played your Semi-final. Waiting for zara_vr.*
- *You're through! The next draw is …*
- *You won Super Over Cup! 🏆*

The card also shows where you play, your opponent, and whether it is **your turn**.

Buttons on the card:

| Button | When |
|---|---|
| **Change where I play** | Before the tournament starts |
| **Leave tournament** | Before the tournament starts |
| **Buy back in** | You were knocked out in round 1 and buybacks are still open |
| **Tournament details** | Always — shows every round and result |

**Join another tournament** at the top starts the three steps again for a new tournament.

## 3. Link your headset (once)

To play in VR, your headset must know who you are.

1. On **My tournaments**, find the **Link Meta Quest** box. It shows a 4-digit code.
2. In the Quest app, enter that code.
3. Done — the headset now greets you by name, shows your tournaments, and every score you play is
   saved to your account.

The code is valid for 10 minutes. If it runs out, tap **Refresh** for a new one. You only need to
do this once per headset.

## 4. How a tournament works

Diagram: [tournament lifecycle](../flows/tournament-lifecycle.md).

1. **Registration** is open until the start time.
2. **At the start**, round 1 is drawn automatically. Everyone gets one opponent. If there is an
   odd number of players, one player plays a computer opponent instead.
3. **Each round** stays open for the set time (for example 3 hours). Play your match any time
   inside it — there is no booking and no fixed time slot.
4. **When the round closes**, winners go through and the next round is drawn straight away. A round
   can also close early once every match in it is finished.
5. **The last player standing** is the champion.

### Playing a match

Diagram: [how a match is decided](../flows/match-and-round.md).

- Each player plays **one innings**.
- Whoever plays first **sets the target**. The other player **chases** it and must score one run
  more to win. The headset shows the number to beat.
- You and your opponent do not have to be online at the same time.

| What happens | Result |
|---|---|
| Both played | Higher score wins. |
| A tie | You both **replay** the match before the round closes. If it is still tied when the round closes, whoever batted first wins. |
| Only one of you played | The player who played goes through. |
| Neither of you played | Both are out. |

**Computer opponent:** if the field is odd, one player is drawn against the computer. It plays
after you and finishes close to your score — sometimes just ahead, sometimes just short.

## 5. Buybacks

If you lose in **round 1**, you may be able to buy your way back in:

- only in round 1, and never in a final,
- only until round 1 closes,
- the price is shown on the tournament card (for example $5),
- pay by card on **My tournaments → Buy back in**.

You then join the **round 2 draw**. If a payment arrives after round 1 has already closed, it is
refunded automatically.

## 6. Playing in more than one tournament

You can join as many open tournaments as you like, each with its own place to play. Every
tournament has its own card and its own match.

In VR, if you have matches in two tournaments, the headset asks you **which match to play** and
shows the tournament name and round on screen, so a score always goes to the right match.

## 7. Notifications

The bell at the top shows everything; the important ones are emailed too (✉).

| When | Message |
|---|---|
| You join | You're in! ✉ |
| 1 hour before the start | The tournament starts soon ✉ |
| A round starts | Who you play, where, and the deadline ✉ |
| Your opponent has played | Their score and what you need to win ✉ |
| The round is nearly over and you haven't played | How long is left ✉ |
| A tie | Replay before the deadline |
| You win / lose | Through to the next round / knocked out (with buyback offer in round 1 ✉) |
| The tournament ends | Who won (the champion also gets an email ✉) |

Tapping a notification takes you to **My tournaments**.

## 8. Your profile

**Profile** (top menu) lets you change your username, city, photo, and whether you have a VR
headset. You cannot switch the headset off while you are playing a tournament from **Home** —
change where you play first, or wait until it finishes.

Your public profile shows your rating, tier, wins and losses.

## 9. Help

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| I can't find a tournament | Only tournaments still taking players are listed. New ones appear as soon as they open. |
| Home isn't offered | Switch on **I have a VR headset** in your profile. |
| The headset says my code is invalid | Codes last 10 minutes and work once. Tap **Refresh** on My tournaments and try again. |
| I missed my match | If your opponent played, they went through. If neither of you played, both are out. |
| I paid for a buyback but I'm not back in | Round 1 had already closed; the payment is refunded automatically. |
